# Code of Conduct

Zynum contributors are expected to keep technical discussion respectful,
focused, and evidence-based.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, repeated
off-topic disruption, publishing private information, or intentionally
misrepresenting benchmark or correctness results.

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions, pull requests, or other project
spaces to protect the quality and safety of the community. Enforcement actions
may include warnings, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately by email to
[kxhuang0@gmail.com](mailto:kxhuang0@gmail.com). Include the relevant URLs or
context, the behavior involved, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will
be handled confidentially and shared only with people needed to investigate and
respond, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to address an
immediate safety risk.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
and do not disclose vulnerability details in public issues or pull requests.
